Where is my data stored?

Active recipes and photos live in PetalPull's private iOS app container. The app has no account or developer-operated cloud sync, but iOS may include the container in device or iCloud backups according to your system settings.

How do I move recipes to another iPhone?

  1. Open Backup & restore.
  2. Tap Export JSON backup and save the file somewhere you control.
  3. On the other device, install PetalPull and choose that backup file.
  4. Review the confirmation before replacing the local library.

Why can't I take a photo?

Camera access is requested only after you choose Take Photo. If access was denied, open Settings → Apps → PetalPull → Camera, or choose an existing image with Apple's system photo picker.

Does PetalPull need my whole photo library?

No. Choose from Library uses Apple's system picker so you provide only the photo you select.

How do I print or share a card?

Open a saved recipe and use the print or PDF-sharing action. PetalPull creates a 5×7-inch card and opens the standard iOS print or share interface.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting PetalPull removes recipes and photos from its active local app container. Export a backup first if you want to keep or move the library. An OS-managed backup may retain or later restore app data according to your iOS settings.
