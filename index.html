Common questions

Is PetalPull a flower ordering system?

PetalPull organizes your recipes and production quantities. It does not place supplier orders, check live stock, or manage client bookings.

Can I combine several designs into one pull sheet?

Yes. Enter a production quantity for each recipe. The app calculates each material line, then combines matching material names and colors.

Can I work without a connection?

Core recipe and production work runs offline. Printing and sharing use the destinations available on your iPhone.

Can I print instructions for the bench?

Yes. Create 5×7 recipe cards and share or print PDFs using the standard iOS interface.
