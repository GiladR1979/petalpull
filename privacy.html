Privacy Policy

PetalPull is a private, local-first iPhone app for floral recipe cards and production pull sheets.

Data collection

PetalPull does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or with third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking services.

The app has no account, login, developer-operated server, cloud synchronization, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, subscription, or in-app purchase SDK.

Data stored by the app

Recipe details, optional reference photos, and app-managed print or backup files are stored as active data in PetalPull's private iOS app container. They are not linked to an account or advertising identifier.

Photos and camera

Choose from Library uses Apple's system photo picker. On supported iOS versions, it lets you provide only the item you select without granting PetalPull broad photo-library access.

Take Photo requests optional camera access. If you deny it, you can continue without a photo or use the system photo picker. Selected or captured images are validated, resized, and copied into private app storage. PetalPull does not upload or synchronize them and does not request microphone access.

Printing, sharing, and backups

Printing, PDF sharing, and JSON backup export occur only after you choose the action. iOS displays its system interface, and the destination you choose may have its own privacy policy. Backups can include embedded recipe photos; keep exported files private if they contain commercially sensitive material.

iOS device and iCloud backups

PetalPull does not operate a cloud service or perform automatic uploads. iOS may nevertheless include the private app container in an encrypted device backup or iCloud Backup according to your system settings. Apple controls that OS-managed backup and restore behavior.

Retention and deletion

Active app data remains until you delete a recipe, replace or remove its photo, restore a replacement backup, or remove PetalPull. Deleting the app removes its active iOS container. Previously exported copies and OS-managed backups are separate; an iOS backup may retain or later restore app data according to your settings.

Children's privacy

PetalPull is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Policy changes

If PetalPull's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed behavior is released.
